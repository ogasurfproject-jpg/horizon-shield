GROK IMAGE BRIEF: social hero for the "pace the frontier" posts (2026-09-14)

WHAT THIS IS FOR
One hero image shared with three posts (LinkedIn EN, Bluesky EN, note JA). The posts say:
the world's fastest AI builders just admitted AI moves too fast to check, and the piece
almost no one names is this: what did an AI agent do, and WHEN, provable without trusting
the company that built it. The proof comes from a clock the operator cannot move, the Bitcoin
block time. The image must carry "provably before or after a fixed moment," and grab attention
as a share card while staying restrained and serious. NOT generic AI tech art.

THE IDEA IN ONE LINE
A timeline. One fixed red moment. A record that lands provably before it, on a clock nobody can move.

WHY NOT A GLOWING BRAIN, ROBOT, OR NEON CLOCK
Those are the exact hype the posts push against. We do the opposite: matte, quiet, exact,
editorial. The restraint is the argument. The picture should feel like a proof, not a promo.

=================================================================
ROUTE A (primary): Swiss typographic poster. Paste this into Grok.
=================================================================
A poster in the tradition of Swiss modernist graphic design, flat vector, strict modular grid,
on a warm off white paper background. A single thin horizontal black line runs left to right
across the lower middle, a bare time axis. One vertical red hairline crosses it cleanly at about
two thirds to the right, and at the top of that red line sits one small solid red square, a fixed
moment. To the LEFT of the red line, one small solid black square sits precisely on the axis, a
single anchored datum, clearly before the red moment. The empty distance between the black square
and the red line is the whole point: leave it open and calm. Only three colors exist: near black,
warm off white, one signal red. Absolutely flat, zero gradients, zero shadows, zero glow, zero
rounded corners, no photography. Enormous negative space above and below the axis. Rational,
confident, striking at thumbnail size.

No text at all. The argument lives in the post, not the picture. The two marks and the gap

HOW TO ITERATE WITH GROK
1. Generate 4 from Route A. Pick the one where the red hairline is thinnest, the two squares are
   smallest and most exact, and the negative space is calmest.
2. If it looks busy or decorative: "make the marks smaller and more exact, this is a serious
   editorial proof poster, not a pattern design. More empty off white."
3. If the red is too thick or too bright: "the red is one thin vertical hairline and one small
   square only, dial it back, everything else stays black and off white."
4. If it adds a clock, coin, brain, robot, glow, or any text: "remove it, keep it flat, matte,
   and empty, just one black line, one red moment, one black point before it."
