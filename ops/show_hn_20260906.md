Show HN: A conduct record for AI agents that the agent did not write (one command to be a witness)

URL: https://github.com/ogasurfproject-jpg/mcp-conduct-register

Text (first comment, post it yourself right after submitting):

Most "trust" signals for MCP servers and A2A agents are written by the agent or by whoever the agent pays: card descriptions, directory scores, vendor pages. I run a small public register where the record is written by a gate the agent does not control, and by anyone else who walks the agent from their own machine.

What exists: a free conformance gate (five conditions, no scores, every verdict carries a sha256), daily re-measurement of listed endpoints, monthly "rings" per endpoint (counts only, hash-chained, the month's list anchored to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps), and an append-only ledger that pools outside witnesses' observations and bundles them daily. Two witnesses who disagree are recorded as a discrepancy, not resolved. The operator's own servers are on the register and measured the same way. A second person wrote a Node.js ring builder from the published spec and ring files; it reproduced all eight August rings byte for byte (ledger entries 34 and 36).

Honest numbers today: 9 endpoints, 1 outside witness, 37 ledger entries. That is why I am posting.

Be a witness in one command (stdlib Python, no account):

    uvx --from "git+https://github.com/ogasurfproject-jpg/horizon-shield#subdirectory=workers/hs-ledger/nenrin/a2a-conduct-walk" a2a-conduct-walk --origin https://mcp.horizonshield.dev --mode a2a --submit --witness-name "you" --vantage "where"

Or as an MCP server with one tool (conduct-witness-mcp) so your agent can be the witness. The record's sha256 is your receipt; it lands in the next day's ledger bundle and the month's ring counts you by name.

What it is not: not a rating, not a lie detector, not a measure of quality. It records shape, the gap between what a card declares and what the endpoint does, and the gap between witnesses. Prior art is named in the spec (CT, Rekor, in-toto, SCITT, ERC-8004, Sigstore agent cards). The spec is Apache-2.0 and its bytes are ledger entry 37.
