One finding that may matter to anyone implementing an extension on the 0.3 compatibility path: I drove the real servers with the official a2a-sdk (Python 1.1.2) and @a2a-js/sdk (1.1.0) clients on both wires, and a server that reads only A2A-Extensions silently drops activation from the JS SDK's 0.3 path, which emits X-A2A-Extensions alone. Reading both spellings and echoing in the spelling received fixed it; the harness is in the repo (workers/hs-mcp/test). Formal proposal, per the governance process, is issue #2211.
