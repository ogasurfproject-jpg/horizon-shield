## A. Paragraphs for paper 2

### A1. New 4.5 (or end of 4.4): the fifth coordinate, who the witness is and what a record claims

The record expected a fifth coordinate; the founding witness's public work supplied it the day after this draft was written. A witness record carries a name, and the name is chosen by the witness. A flood of self-named records can therefore fill the disagreement column of a ring without any of them being attributable, which is the prover choosing the coordinate one more time, on the witness side. The revision of the conduct extension served since 2026-09-07 (conduct-v1.1, section 11 of the text at the same URI, every added field optional, spec_markdown_sha256 0881a07c5a653e9ab358b77155ee46a57a39930451e51b0561b0f7f4693242c5) applies the rule again: a witness MAY sign the record (Ed25519 over the canonical bytes, the intake's existing signature) and name `witness.key_url`, an https URL under a domain it controls that serves the same public key; the identity is then the domain that serves the key and never the string in the record; a key served from the walked agent's own domain is refused as `self_witness`; and the ring counts signed and unsigned witnesses in two columns with no score in either. Unsigned records are stored, counted by the name given, and the ring's limits say so. Storage and counting are separated: the intake stores every schema-valid record inside its lanes (5 per address per UTC day unsigned, 50 per domain signed, 500 per day in total) and marks the first record per identity, endpoint and day as counted; later ones are stored, anchored, and not counted, so the cap hides nothing from the record while stopping one address from filling a column alone.

The same revision adopts a discipline the founding witness reported publicly on 2026-09-07 from a different specification, a TEE attestation conformance suite in which a leaf could silently drop its "not judged correct" disclaimer and still pass, repaired the same day by making the disclaimer a required field with a conformance vector for its omission. The witness records here had the same defect: the sentence "a PASS is one observation, not a verdict" lived in the specification's prose and nowhere in the record. conduct-v1.1 makes `establishes` and `does_not_establish` required fields of a v1.1 record, refuses a record that omits or empties either (`disclaimer_missing`), and holds a red team vector for the omission. The operator's own gate verdicts were given the same two fields in the same release (gate 0.4.0, commit 74fd08d2 at 2026-09-06T16:15:54Z, deployed minutes later; the subrequest budget of A3 followed as commit 082d6834 at 16:34:02Z), inside the bytes that `record_sha256` hashes: a quotation of a verdict with the two arrays removed no longer recomputes. The operator is a subject and not an exception, and the change was made on the measurer's side first, before any witness was asked to make it.

### A3. Limitation to state (section 8 or wherever limits are listed)

The gate runs on a Workers plan that allows 50 outbound fetches per invocation. The first sweep of a window pays 7 for the beacon (three sources) and 2 for the commitment filings before any row is measured, and a consented row with a signed card costs about 6 (initialize, tools/list, card, jwks, two tool calls). On 2026-09-06 that left room for 7 of the 8 rows; the eighth (femtech) was not measured and the sweep record says so, with `subrequests: {used: 47, budget: 50}` and the row listed under skipped with the reason. The row goes first at the next sweep. Until 0.4.0 the gate did not count its own fetches and would have aborted mid-measurement on that sweep, leaving a partial instant in the history; the count was added the same evening, before the sweep. A skipped row is an instant not sampled, disclosed; a partial instant would have been a false one.

### A6. Optional, for the discussion (section 10): the judgement the operator refused to add

On 2026-09-07 the operator asked for a private detector that would activate on suspicion of a false declaration. It was refused, by the operator's own reviewer and then by the operator, for the reason this paper gives everywhere else: a judgement whose trigger, timing and inputs are not public is a coordinate the operator controls, and one such coordinate discredits every public one. What is planned instead (0.4.1) is contradiction detection under published rules only: a version that does not move when the tool surface does, a card citing a verdict hash that the register does not hold, two compensation declarations that disagree, a disclosure URL that does not answer, a body hash that differs between two vantages in the same window, an output that violates the declared schema. Each becomes a counted field in the record, never a verdict, and none counts until its rule has been published.

## D. What changed between the draft (v1 of this note) and what shipped

1. Signing is Ed25519 plus witness.key_url, not ES256 JWS with sig, kid, jwks_url.
2. Caps are lanes: 5 per address per day, 50 per domain, 500 total; the draft said 200 per address.
3. notify, identity and witness_policy live in the origin's consent file (/.well-known/mcp-conduct.json), which only the owner can place; the draft put them in the card.
4. notify fires after every scheduled measurement, not only on change; once per hour per endpoint; at most 3 per sweep; never from /check; never to an IP literal or a local name.
5. Commitment mode declares commitment_recipe; the gate uses it to anchor the instant coordinate salt (A2).
6. Ring columns from 2026-09 if two builders agree, not from October.
7. The gate's verdicts and /self carry the two arrays inside the hash; GET /register/lookup is live (24 hour edge cache, no KV writes).
8. The subrequest budget (A3) exists; it did not in the draft and it would have broken the first derived sweep.
9. The v1 wording "not yet served or anchored" is false now: served at the URI with sha 0881a07c…; the text itself is not yet a separate ledger entry (the v1 text is entry 37). Anchoring section 11 as its own entry is TOshi's call; the paper can cite the sha either way.
