Measurement is free. Two hours of attention is not.

Being measured, being listed, the badge, the generic fix for every condition, and asking for a re-measurement are all free and stay free. What has a price is labour: somebody looking at your specific server, or turning a measurement into a document a decision maker will sign.

Correction · 2026-08-19

Earlier on 2026-08-19 this page carried a different headline, and it was live for several hours. It said, in full:

If you run a server, there is nothing here to buy.
Measurement is free, the badge is free, the fix instructions are free, and being on the register is free. None of that has a paid tier and none of it is going to grow one. The paid work is on the other side of the connection, for companies deciding what to plug into.

That is no longer true. There is now one paid thing for operators: a report on why your specific server did not meet a condition. It is here, and it names the conflict of interest on its own front page.

This is a change of policy, not a corrected measurement. It is printed the same way anyway, because a price list that quietly changes is not a price list, and because the earlier wording was published and somebody may have read it.

Free, permanently

The things that would be worth the most to charge for

measurement
Any server, any time, no account. Including servers you do not own. Read only, no tool calls.
the register
Being listed. And staying listed when a row is not green, which is the part that makes the green ones mean anything.
the badge
Live and downloadable. The saved file carries the measurement date, so it cannot quietly outlive the measurement.
fix instructions
What to change for each condition, in plain words, on the operators page. Complete, not a teaser. The paid version is not more of this text; it is somebody reading the bytes your endpoint actually returned. The difference, priced.

the survey data

The generic fix for every condition is free and complete. This is for when you have followed it and your row is still not green. The raw response we got, the exact condition, what to change, and a re-measurement after.

Why the audit is priced in Japanese yen and described in Japanese: the buyer is a Japanese company putting a document in front of a decision maker, and a document in English does not survive that meeting. The measurement underneath is the same machine measuring the same five conditions.
Not for sale

There is no price for these because there is no mechanism for these

  • ✗A verdict. You cannot buy a pass. There is no code path that takes a payment and changes a status.
  • ✗Removal of a row. A row that is not green stays visible. We did not build a delete.
  • ✗Position in the list. Order is not sold and there is no sponsored slot.
  • ✗Referral fees. Zero. Nobody listed here pays us for being listed here.

Our own servers are measured by the same machine on the same schedule, and some of them are not green right now. If a payment could have fixed that, it would have.
