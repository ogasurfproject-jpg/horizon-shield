We measured you, we published that you did not pass, and now we are selling you the fix.

That is a conflict of interest, and naming it is not a defence. So here is what was done about it, in the machine rather than in this paragraph.

The verdict is produced by a machine and there is no code path that takes a payment and changes a status. Not buying this changes nothing about your row. Buying it changes nothing about your row either. Only the next measurement does.

The free path is complete, not a teaser. What to change for each of the five conditions is published in full on the operators page, and asking for a re-measurement is free forever. Most operators should stop there and never come here.

The price is arithmetic you can check. Hours times a published rate, with the hours itemised below. It is not a number chosen by looking at how badly you want the row to be green.

Before you pay anything

Three free things that solve most of it

  1. Read what the condition actually measures

    Each of the five is a specific request and a specific field, written out. Most rows that are not green are one missing key in one JSON file. The five conditions.

  2. Recompute our verdict yourself

    If our hash does not reproduce, the defect is ours and fixing it is our job, not something you should be paying for. Recompute.

  3. Ask for a re-measurement

    Free, no account, and you do not have to own the server. If you already changed something, this is all you need. The corrections desk.

If those three got you to green, you are done. That is the intended outcome and it costs nothing. The paid work below exists for the case where you have done all three and your row is still not green, and you would rather buy two hours of somebody else's attention than spend a day on it.

Condition 06 · the newest finding, and the work that clears it

Can an agent tell your failure from your silence?

The five conditions above are pass or fail. Condition 06 is not. It is a disclosed measurement, free and with no verdict: from tools/list alone, does each tool's declared outputSchema let a consumer separate “the call failed” from “the call found nothing”? Across the public field, 96.7 to 99.1% of tools cannot, and the field names behind every score are published so you can see the false positives yourself. The free measurement is on the operators page.

This is the paid work that changes your number, and it is engineering, not a mark. A real read-state where none exists (a boolean, or an enum such as lookup: ok | absent); failures moved into the tool-error channel (isError) instead of riding home as a successful-looking result; the correct shape carried across every tool rather than one and not its neighbour. We measure your number before and after, under one ruler, in public, with the gate as the witness.

The same rails apply as everywhere here. Condition 06 is a measurement, not a pass, so there is nothing to buy that changes a verdict. We do not send this offer to the 12,429 endpoints we measured; you came to the free number, and the offer is here for when you want the next step. And a schema is a declaration, not behaviour: we fix the declared contract and the runtime path behind it, and where only tools/call would prove the runtime, we say so rather than claim it.

What this does not buy

Stated at the same size as the price

  • ✗Not a passing verdict. There is no mechanism. The next measurement decides, and it does not know whether you paid.
  • ✗Not removal of the row you have now. The history stays, including the stretch where it was not green. Ours does too.
  • ✗Not priority. There is no faster queue, because there is no queue. Measurement is automatic and free for everybody at the same speed.
  • ✗Not silence. Your row says what it says whether you buy this or not.
