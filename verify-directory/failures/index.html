Two words that are not the same word

Held is not failed

verified
Measured, and every condition that was measured was met.
pending
We measured it and a condition was not met. This is a statement about the server.
held
We could not measure it. This is a statement about our instrument, not about the server. On 2026-08-19 our own gate spent a stretch of days in this state because of a fault entirely on our side, and it was labelled held the whole time rather than being labelled unreachable.

If a row here is held, the honest reading is "unknown", and it would be wrong to tell anyone otherwise.
