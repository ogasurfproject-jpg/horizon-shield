Thank you. Two things left, and then we start measuring.

You have paid for cadence and for being told. You have not paid for a verdict, and nothing you just did moves a single condition. Here is how to tell us what to watch.

Your PayPal subscription id

1. Tell us what to watch

Up to three endpoints on this subscription. One command each, no account, no key.

curl -s -X POST https://gate.horizonshield.dev/watch \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"endpoint":"https://your-server/mcp",
       "webhook":"https://your-endpoint-for-alerts"}'

The webhook is optional but it is most of what you are paying for. It must be https. We POST a small JSON to it when a condition actually flips, and never otherwise. We never send it anything about your users, because we never see anything about your users.

You can check what we hold for an endpoint at any time:

curl -s "https://gate.horizonshield.dev/watch?endpoint=https://your-server/mcp"

2. Send us the subscription id

Registration alone puts you on the free weekly cadence. To switch you to daily measurement and alerts, we need to connect your payment to your endpoints.

Email contact@the-horizons-innovation.com with your subscription id and the endpoints you registered. Subject line: monitoring.

This step is manual today, and we would rather say so than pretend

You are among the first paying subscribers, so a person reads your email and flips the tier by hand. It takes minutes, not days. We will automate it when the volume justifies it, and not before, because a payment webhook written for nobody is a payment webhook nobody has tested.

Until the tier is switched, your endpoints are already being measured weekly and the history is already public. Nothing is lost in the gap.最初の購読者なので、ここは人が手で切り替えます。切り替わるまでの間も週1回の測定と公開履歴は動いています。

What happens after that

  1. Measured every day. The sweep calls no tool on your server. It reads your endpoint and your agent card, nothing else.
  2. A webhook when a condition flips, with the condition named and the reason in plain words.
  3. No alert on a single unreachable check. Three consecutive unreachable measurements before anything is sent. A network blip is not a failure.
  4. The history stays public, at /history?endpoint=..., whether the news is good or bad. Paying means you hear first, not that nobody else hears.
  5. Cancel any time from your PayPal account. Your public history stays, because deleting a record on request would make every record worthless.
