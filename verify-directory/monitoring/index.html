What you are not paying for

Every line here is enforced in the machine readable disclosure, not only in this sentence.

  • Not a passing verdict. The check is deterministic. Paying does not move a single condition, and there is no human who could move it for you.
  • Not a listing. Appearing in the directory is free, and nobody pays for position.
  • Not silence. If your endpoint fails, the public record says so whether you pay or not. Paying means you hear about it first, not that nobody else does.
  • Not a certificate. A verdict is a statement about a measurement taken at a time. It expires by being re-measured, which is exactly what you are buying.

Mould records

Whether the class was searched, not whether the instance was fixed

A fix repairs one casting. The mould that cast it sits untouched unless somebody goes looking. This ledger records four things about a fix and freezes them: the class of assumption behind it, where the author searched for that same assumption, what they found at each location, and at what volume each casting failed. Append only. Every record carries a SHA-256 you can recompute yourself.

A record with an empty search list is accepted and published as such. An instance fixed with no class search is the exact thing this ledger exists to make visible. It is not rejected and it is not hidden. Those records are marked below.

This ledger verifies nothing. Each record says so in its own body: it is the author's own account, this gate did not reproduce it, and what is frozen is the claim and its date, not its truth.
