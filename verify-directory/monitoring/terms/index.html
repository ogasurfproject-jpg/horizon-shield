1. Who may buy

Monitoring is offered to businesses and other organizations, for business use. It is not offered to consumers. By subscribing, you confirm that you act for a business and may accept these terms for it.

2. What Monitoring is

  • Each endpoint you register is measured once a day against the published conditions of the verification gate, as listed on the monitoring page and returned by the gate's get_conditions tool.
  • When a condition changes state, we send an alert to the webhook URL or email address you gave us, stating which condition changed and why. We aim to send it within one hour of the measurement. An endpoint is reported as unreachable only after three consecutive failed measurements.
  • Each measurement produces a public record. Records are batched into the NENRIN ledger and timestamped in Bitcoin through OpenTimestamps.
  • Plans:
    • Standard: up to 3 endpoints, USD 149 per month or USD 1,490 per year.
    • Pro: up to 10 endpoints, USD 490 per month or USD 4,900 per year.
    • The price shown on https://shield.the-horizons-innovation.com/verify-directory/monitoring/ when you subscribe applies.

3. What Monitoring is not

A measurement records how an endpoint answered at a point in time against stated conditions. It is not a security audit, a penetration test, a certification, or a promise that an endpoint is safe, lawful or fit for any purpose. A signature shows who published a record, and a timestamp shows that it existed by a given time. Neither shows that the subject of the record can be trusted. You remain responsible for your own systems and for the decisions you make with the records.

4. Your endpoints

  • Register only endpoints that you operate, or that you have permission to have measured. You confirm this for each endpoint you register.
  • Our measurements read only. We fetch public metadata and, only where you allow it, request the tool list. We use no credentials, and we send no more than a few requests per endpoint per day.
  • You may change your registered endpoints at any time by email, within the limit of your plan.

5. Public records

Records that anyone can recompute are the point of the service. By registering an endpoint, you agree that its measurement records are published and kept in the public ledger. A record holds the endpoint URL, the verdict, the conditions measured and the hash of the record.

Once a record has been batched and timestamped, it cannot be changed or removed, because removing it would break the ledger for everyone who relies on it. When you cancel, we stop measuring. We do not delete past records. Do not register an endpoint whose URL must stay confidential.

Your webhook URL, your contact details and your billing details are never published.
