The order this happens in

Read this before the button. If you have arrived here without step 3, you are early, and paying now would put money in front of work nobody has agreed to yet.

  1. You send the endpoint. No account, no charge. contact@the-horizons-innovation.com
  2. We look, at no charge. If the free pages solve it, we tell you that and stop. This is the step that most often ends it.
  3. We quote. Hours times a printed rate, with the number of endpoints named. Nothing is charged until you agree to that quote.
  4. You pay here. Outside Japan, the quoted amount is paid before the work starts. In Japan it is invoiced after delivery, by bank transfer.
  5. We deliver, then re-measure. The re-measurement is included. If our instructions do not work, we do the whole thing again once at no charge.
