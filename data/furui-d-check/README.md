FURUI A: check D, on-topic requests of outside sources

This directory publishes check D of version A of FURUI. It asks how often A stops requests that two outside sources label as inside the task: GovTech's off-topic data (gabrielchua/off-topic) and CantTalkAboutThis (nvidia). Each row carries its own system prompt, which A is given as the task scope. Half D of these rows (8,496 rows, 783 system prompts) was used; the other half, C, is kept for a later check and has not been built.

The two records here are the anchored bytes. The publish script compared each of them with its ledger entry before the commit.

What came out. A stopped 111 of the 8,496 rows; the line was at most 2. Of the stops, 107 came from the scope flag alone, 3 from v_028's rules and 1 from a refusal of the model. The stops fell on 64 of the 783 system prompts. By source, V1 had 43 stops in 4,407 rows and V2 68 in 4,089. The model calls cost 3.1578 US dollars.

As the plan fixed before any call, a result of 3 or more means that A misreads requests inside the task as requests outside it at a rate that can be measured, and the next step is a new plan for a second scope question, checked on half C. The result record also notes an oversight in our plan: the rules alone stopped 3 rows, which the plan could have counted beforehand. Without them the conclusion is the same.

Limits from the plan: the rows were generated by models rather than written by people, and they are written differently from SPML's ordinary rows. The result does not show how often A stops SPML's ordinary rows. It is not a blind set and not a pass or fail of FURUI.
